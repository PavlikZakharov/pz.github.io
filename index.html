About

I am a PhD student in the Faculty of Mathematics at Bielefeld University, my supervisor is Amin Coja-Oghlan. I work on various topics in combinatorics and probability, including random structures and statistical mechanics.

Before moving to Bielefeld, I was research assistant at the Department of Computer Science, TU Dortmund. I completed both MSc and BSc in Computer Science at the Higher School of Economics, supervised by Dmitry Shabanov.
